01 / Research

  1. 01

    Axions &
    wave dark matter

    I develop quantum descriptions of wave dark matter and its measurements, and study the observability of its quantum properties. My interests also include the theoretical origins and phenomenology of axions and axion-like particles.

  2. 02

    Inflation &
    cosmic relics

    I investigate models of inflation and reheating and their connections to primordial black holes and stochastic gravitational waves. I also investigate cosmological perturbations and their consequences for dark matter physics.

  3. 03

    BSM phenomenology

    I study a wide range of BSM phenomenology and particle cosmology. These include gravitational effects such as axion wormholes, nonminimal gravitational couplings, and theoretical consistency bounds.
